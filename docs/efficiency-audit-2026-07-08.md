## 3. WHAT NOT TO TOUCH

The evidence says these layers earn their cost; several were the sole catcher of a shipped-quality bug.

1. **Full-scope adversarial codex on security-sensitive scopes (gates/matchers/parsers/hooks), every round.** All historical settled-scope BLOCKINGs live here (regress r4/r5/r6; todo-triage rounds 3–5 matcher bypasses; c7's 109-min WorktreeCreate fail-open). D9/D12 raised the cap 2→8 precisely because "codex was simply correct" — the rounds exist because the hunt keeps being right, and OPERATING.md makes this voice load-bearing on this code class, which is most of this repo.
2. **The phase-integration fresh review, even on single-slice fast-forward.** Caught the heal adopt-path BLOCKING after 7 clean slice rounds. R11 keeps a fresh codex pass there for exactly this reason.
3. **The forced next-invocation fresh dual-voice re-audit after any logic-bearing fix round.** Retention harden-2-3: fix-introduced P1 found after the regress review had CONVERGED at the post-fix tip with a green suite. The regress lens and bug-hunt lens are different prompts; merge them (R10), never drop one.
4. **Finalize's whole-run fresh sweep.** Retention finalize r2 found a TOCTOU P1 outside the previous round's fix delta in a destructive script. Finalize stays out of every diet.
5. **The fresh Claude dispatch after a prior-CONVERGED round on design/spec scopes.** Five historical catches codex missed: lever2 4.5-r3 (inert regex fix), 4.5-r5 (adjacent-input-class hole), 3.1-r3 (spec-doc omission), regress pd1-r3 (AC11 undercount), main pd2-r5 (empirically refuted calibration claims). Design docs have no mutation-verify surface — the human-like re-read is the only check.
6. **Seam A/B themselves (fresh-context-per-leg + decant-at-clear).** Keep them unconditional; R1 automates only the paste, not the seam. The class-A pressure rebirth has never fired in production and must not become the only guard.
7. **Gate A / Gate B human-only semantics, and repaste-is-not-approval.** Gate B authorizes `git push` + PR creation; no notification, watcher, or resume mechanism may carry an approval-shaped paste line (R3 constraint 2).
8. **The SHA-bound omission-proof artifact chain and cap-8 counters.** No recommendation above forges, resets, or bypasses a counter or a SHA-bound artifact; the enforcement threat model (omission-proof, not forgery-proof) is unchanged. D12's cap is a runaway detector — revision legs and delta rounds are accounted inside it, not around it.
9. **The overrule-with-evidence discipline.** Count codex severity tags, not prose; a refutation requires a faithful-env executed reproduction; an executed red always defeats a ledger entry. The one near-shipped bug in the corpus (c7 TMPDIR) came from relaxing exactly this.

## Net effect

For the complained-of run shape (1000-line change, 1–2 phases): R1+R3 remove 3–8h of pure human latency; R2+R4 remove 0.5–1.5h of serialization and bound the multi-hour codex tail to minutes; R5–R9 remove 1.5–3h of round churn without touching any layer that has ever been the sole catcher. A run that today spans a full day of wall clock compresses to an attended morning: ~4–6h machine-active with same-day human touches only at Gate A, Gate B, and genuine STOPs. Implementation order: R2 (one afternoon, pure spec), then R1+R3 (the latency pool), then R4, then the churn set R5–R9 as one spec-edit batch (single pin-suite migration), with R10–R12 opportunistic and R11's shed (1) parked behind the TR-9 re-measure.
